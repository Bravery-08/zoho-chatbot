SOP-001: CUSTOMER COMPLAINT HANDLING
----------------------------------------------------------------

PURPOSE:
To ensure all customer complaints are handled promptly, professionally,
and consistently across all support channels.

SCOPE:
Applies to all customer-facing staff including support agents,
account managers, and team leads.

PROCEDURE:

Step 1 — Acknowledge the Complaint
- Respond to the customer within 2 hours of receiving the complaint.
- Use the customer's name and acknowledge their frustration.
- Do not argue, interrupt, or become defensive.
- Example opening: "Thank you for bringing this to our attention,
  [Name]. I understand how frustrating this must be."

Step 2 — Log the Complaint
- Open a new ticket in the CRM system (Freshdesk).
- Set the Priority field: Low / Medium / High / Critical.
  - Critical: Service outage, data loss, legal threat.
  - High: Payment issue, repeated failure, VIP customer.
  - Medium: Feature not working, delay in service.
  - Low: General inquiry or minor inconvenience.
- Tag the ticket with the appropriate category:
  Billing / Technical / Delivery / Conduct / Other.

Step 3 — Investigate
- Gather all relevant information: order ID, account number,
  screenshots, timestamps.
- Check internal systems for error logs or transaction history.
- Do not promise a resolution timeline you cannot commit to.
- Escalate to Tier 2 support if unresolved within 24 hours.

Step 4 — Resolve and Respond
- Communicate the resolution clearly and in plain language.
- Offer compensation if applicable (see SOP-007: Refund Policy).
- Confirm the customer is satisfied before closing the ticket.

Step 5 — Close and Document
- Mark ticket as Resolved only after customer confirmation.
- Add internal notes summarizing the root cause and fix.
- If the complaint reveals a systemic issue, flag it to the
  Operations Manager for review.

ESCALATION PATH:
Agent → Team Lead → Operations Manager → Director of Customer Success

KPI TARGETS:
- First Response Time: < 2 hours
- Resolution Time (Low/Medium): < 48 hours
- Resolution Time (High/Critical): < 8 hours
- Customer Satisfaction Score (CSAT): > 85%

SOP-002: EMPLOYEE ONBOARDING PROCESS
----------------------------------------------------------------

PURPOSE:
To provide a structured and welcoming experience for new employees
that enables them to become productive within their first 30 days.

SCOPE:
HR Department, Department Managers, and IT Team.

PRE-JOINING (1 week before start date):

- HR sends the offer letter, NDA, and policy documents for signing.
- IT creates company email account and grants system access.
- Manager prepares a 30-day onboarding plan.
- Assign a buddy from the same team to the new employee.
- Send a welcome email with the first-day schedule and office address.

DAY 1:

Morning (9:00 AM – 12:00 PM):
- Welcome meeting with HR: office tour, culture overview, policies.
- Collect signed documents: NDA, code of conduct, bank details form.
- Issue company laptop, access badge, and equipment checklist.
- Set up workstation with the IT team.

Afternoon (1:00 PM – 5:00 PM):
- Introduction meeting with the direct manager.
- Team introduction call or in-person meet.
- Walkthrough of tools: Slack, Jira, Google Workspace, Freshdesk.
- Review of the 30-day onboarding plan.

WEEK 1:
- Shadow at least 2 experienced team members.
- Complete mandatory training modules (listed in the LMS system).
- Attend team standup meetings daily.
- End of week check-in with HR and direct manager.

DAYS 8–30:
- Begin handling assigned tasks with buddy support.
- Weekly 1-on-1 with manager to track progress.
- Complete role-specific training certifications.
- Day 30 review meeting: assess performance, address concerns,
  set 60-day goals.

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Signed offer letter
- Government-issued ID (passport or Aadhaar)
- PAN Card copy
- Bank account details
- Educational certificates (originals for verification)
- 2 passport-size photographs

RESPONSIBLE PARTIES:
- HR Manager: paperwork, system accounts, onboarding schedule.
- IT Team: laptop setup, software installation, email creation.
- Direct Manager: role training, 30-day plan, mentorship.
- Buddy: day-to-day support, culture guidance.

SOP-003: REFUND AND CANCELLATION POLICY
----------------------------------------------------------------

PURPOSE:
To define clear and fair guidelines for processing customer
refunds and cancellations.

SCOPE:
Billing team, support agents, and account managers.

ELIGIBILITY FOR REFUND:

Full Refund (100%):
- Service not delivered within the committed timeframe.
- Duplicate payment made by the customer.
- Technical error on the company's side causing service failure.
- Cancellation requested within 24 hours of purchase (cooling-off period).

Partial Refund (50%):
- Cancellation requested between 24 hours and 7 days of purchase.
- Service partially delivered but incomplete due to company error.

No Refund:
- Cancellation requested after 7 days of purchase.
- Service fully delivered and confirmed by the customer.
- Violation of terms of service by the customer.
- Custom or bespoke projects once work has commenced.

REFUND PROCESS:

Step 1: Customer submits a refund request via support ticket or email.
Step 2: Agent verifies eligibility using the criteria above.
Step 3: If eligible, agent raises a refund request in the billing portal.
Step 4: Billing team approves and processes within 3–5 business days.
Step 5: Customer is notified via email with the transaction reference.
Step 6: Refund reflects in the original payment method within
        7–10 business days depending on the bank.

ESCALATION:
- Disputed refund decisions go to the Billing Manager.
- Refunds above ₹50,000 require Operations Director approval.

COMPENSATION GUIDELINES:
- Delay in service > 48 hours: 10% credit on next invoice.
- Delay in service > 1 week: 25% credit or partial refund option.
- Repeated failures (3+ incidents): escalate to account review.

SOP-004: INCIDENT AND OUTAGE MANAGEMENT
----------------------------------------------------------------

PURPOSE:
To minimize the impact of technical incidents and ensure rapid,
coordinated response and communication.

SEVERITY LEVELS:

SEV-1 (Critical):
- Complete service outage affecting all users.
- Data breach or security compromise.
- Response time: Immediate (within 15 minutes).

SEV-2 (High):
- Major feature unavailable, affecting > 30% of users.
- Significant performance degradation.
- Response time: Within 1 hour.

SEV-3 (Medium):
- Minor feature outage affecting < 30% of users.
- Workaround available.
- Response time: Within 4 hours.

SEV-4 (Low):
- Cosmetic issues, minor bugs, no impact on usability.
- Response time: Within 24 hours.

INCIDENT RESPONSE STEPS:

Step 1 — Detection and Alerting
- Incident detected via monitoring tool (Datadog/PagerDuty) or
  reported by a customer.
- On-call engineer acknowledges the alert within 15 minutes.

Step 2 — Declare Incident
- Create an incident channel in Slack: #incident-YYYY-MM-DD-brief-name
- Assign roles: Incident Commander, Technical Lead, Comms Lead.

Step 3 — Investigate and Mitigate
- Technical Lead identifies root cause and applies immediate fix
  or rollback.
- Incident Commander coordinates efforts and removes blockers.

Step 4 — Customer Communication
- Comms Lead posts update on the status page within 30 minutes.
- For SEV-1 and SEV-2: email affected customers every 1 hour.
- Template: "We are aware of an issue affecting [service]. Our team
  is actively investigating. Next update in 1 hour."

Step 5 — Resolution
- Confirm fix is stable for at least 30 minutes before declaring resolved.
- Update status page to "All Systems Operational."
- Notify affected customers of resolution and root cause summary.

Step 6 — Post-Incident Review (PIR)
- Conduct PIR within 48 hours for SEV-1 and SEV-2.
- Document: timeline, root cause, impact, fix, and preventive actions.
- Share PIR report with the leadership team.

SOP-005: LEAVE AND ATTENDANCE POLICY
----------------------------------------------------------------

PURPOSE:
To outline the types of leave available to employees and the
process for applying and approving leave.

LEAVE ENTITLEMENTS (per calendar year):

- Casual Leave (CL):       8 days
- Sick Leave (SL):         8 days
- Earned Leave (EL):       15 days (accrued at 1.25 days/month)
- Public Holidays:         As per the published holiday calendar
- Maternity Leave:         26 weeks (as per Maternity Benefit Act)
- Paternity Leave:         5 days
- Bereavement Leave:       3 days (immediate family only)
- Marriage Leave:          3 days (once during employment)

RULES:

- Casual Leave cannot be carried forward to the next year.
- Sick Leave requires a medical certificate for absences > 2 days.
- Earned Leave can be carried forward up to a maximum of 30 days.
- Leave cannot be taken without prior approval except in emergencies.
- A minimum of 2 working days' notice is required for CL and EL.

APPLICATION PROCESS:

Step 1: Employee applies via the HRMS portal (Zoho People).
Step 2: System notifies the reporting manager via email.
Step 3: Manager approves or rejects within 1 business day.
Step 4: Approved leave is reflected in the attendance system.
Step 5: For emergency leave, inform manager via Slack/call first,
        then apply through portal on the next working day.

ATTENDANCE:
- Core working hours: 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM (Monday to Friday).
- Flexible window: Employees may start between 9:00 AM and 11:00 AM.
- Late arrival more than 3 times in a month triggers a warning.
- 3 consecutive absent days without notification = disciplinary action.

WORK FROM HOME (WFH):
- Up to 2 WFH days per week (with manager approval).
- WFH not permitted during the first 90 days of employment.
- Employees must be reachable and attend all scheduled meetings.

SOP-006: DATA PRIVACY AND SECURITY POLICY
----------------------------------------------------------------

PURPOSE:
To protect customer and company data in compliance with applicable
data protection laws (IT Act 2000 and DPDP Act 2023).

EMPLOYEE RESPONSIBILITIES:

- Never share login credentials with colleagues or third parties.
- Lock your screen when stepping away from your workstation.
- Do not store customer data on personal devices or USB drives.
- Report any suspected data breach immediately to the IT Security team.
- Use only company-approved tools for communication and file sharing.

PASSWORD POLICY:
- Minimum 12 characters, including uppercase, lowercase,
  numbers, and symbols.
- Passwords must be changed every 90 days.
- Do not reuse the last 5 passwords.
- Use the company password manager (1Password) for all credentials.

DATA CLASSIFICATION:

Confidential:
- Customer PII (name, contact, payment info)
- Employee personal records
- Financial statements
- Strategy documents

Internal:
- Internal emails and reports
- Product roadmaps
- Meeting notes

Public:
- Marketing content
- Published case studies
- Job postings

ACCESS CONTROL:
- Access to systems is granted on a need-to-know basis.
- New access requests must be approved by the department head.
- Access is revoked within 24 hours of an employee's last working day.
- Quarterly access audits are conducted by the IT team.

INCIDENT REPORTING:
- Suspected breach → report to it-security@brightserve.com immediately.
- IT team investigates within 4 hours.
- If breach is confirmed, affected customers are notified within 72 hours
  as required by law.
